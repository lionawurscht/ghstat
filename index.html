Hospital scores

Thresholds and weights
  • Case: one case at one hospital. Completed: a cost was recorded. The cost sorts it into ER / outpatient (below the threshold) or inpatient.
  • No cost: older than the minimum age and still without a cost. Pending: younger than the minimum age; left out of every rate, completed or not, so recent months are not judged before costs can arrive.
  • Success: completed share of the cases older than the minimum age, each weighted by its age (half-life), pulled towards the overall rate for hospitals with few cases (prior weight).
  • Recent: the success rate of the cases in the recent window just past the minimum age, against all older ones. ▼ / ▲ when the two differ by at least the alert threshold, with at least 2 cases on each side.
  • Days to payment: from the service date to the payment, a weighted median over completed cases. A median at or below "fast" scores 1, at or above "slow" 0.
  • Score: success and payment speed, weighted, 0 to 100. The ER and inpatient scores use the same success rate (a case without a cost has no type) and that type's payment speed.
